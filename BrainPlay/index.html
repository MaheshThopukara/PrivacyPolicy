🔒 Privacy Policy

BrainPlay — Educational Games for Kids (Android TV)

Last Updated: September 5, 2026
🛡️ Privacy at a Glance BrainPlay is made for children aged 3–13, so it is built to hold as little as possible. There are no accounts, no advertisements, no analytics and no tracking of any kind. Everything about your child — their name, age, avatar, scores, stars and badges — is stored only on your device and is never uploaded. The app talks to our servers for one reason: to download the questions and pictures the games are made of. That traffic only ever comes down, never up.

1. What We Collect

We do not collect any personal information. BrainPlay has no servers that receive your data, and specifically the app:

  • Does not require an account, sign-in, or email address
  • Does not contain advertisements or any advertising SDK
  • Does not use analytics, telemetry, or crash-reporting services
  • Does not use the advertising ID, or any cross-app or cross-site identifier
  • Does not upload your child's name, age, photos, voice, scores or progress
  • Does not ask for camera, microphone, location, contacts or file access
ℹ️ The only Android permissions BrainPlay requests are INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — enough to download game content and to notice when the device is offline, and nothing else.

2. What Is Stored On Your Device

A grown-up sets up a profile for each child. All of it stays in the app's private storage on your television, and none of it is transmitted:

WhatWhy it exists
Child's first name, age and avatar emojiTo greet them and to choose questions at the right level
Scores, stars, badges, medals and daily-quest progressTo show progress and unlock rewards
Per-child settings (sound, music, read-aloud, calm mode, high contrast)To keep the app the way each child likes it
Screen-time limits, allowed hours and today's usageFor the parental controls a grown-up sets
Downloaded questions and picturesSo games work offline and load quickly
The parental PINTo protect the grown-up area
🔐 The parental PIN is never stored in plain text. We keep a one-way SHA-256 hash combined with a random 16-byte salt generated on your device, plus a lockout after repeated wrong attempts.

3. What Leaves Your Device

BrainPlay uses Google Firebase to host the questions and pictures the games are made of. The app only ever reads from it — there is no code path in the app that writes, uploads or sends anything about your child to any server. Three things travel over the network:

  • Game content requests. The app asks for questions suited to an age band, and that request contains a number — for example 7. It carries no name, no account, no device identifier and nothing that identifies your child, and the age is not stored on the server.
  • Downloads. Question text and game pictures come down to your device and are cached there so the games keep working offline.
  • App verification. Firebase App Check uses Google Play Integrity to confirm the request is coming from a genuine, unmodified copy of BrainPlay, so our content cannot be scraped or abused. This is handled by Google Play services on your device and involves no information about your child.
ℹ️ Because Firebase is a Google service, Google processes these requests as our service provider. Their handling is described in the Firebase Privacy and Security documents.

4. Children's Privacy

BrainPlay is designed for children aged 3 to 13 and is intended to be set up by a parent or guardian. We take the position that the safest way to protect a child's data is not to collect it:

  • No personal information about a child is ever transmitted or stored on our servers
  • No behavioural profile is built, and no data is used for advertising or personalisation beyond choosing questions at the right level, which happens entirely on the device
  • There is no chat, no messaging, no user-generated content and no way to contact anyone through the app
  • There are no external links, in-app purchases or advertisements a child could tap

The name and age a grown-up enters are for the app's own use on that television. They are visible only on that device, and removing the profile removes them.

5. Your Control and Deletion

Because everything is stored locally, you are in complete control:

  • Delete a child's profile in the grown-up area — their name, age, scores, stars, badges and settings are permanently removed from the device
  • Clear app data in Android settings — every stored item is erased
  • Uninstall the app — Android removes all of it with the app

We hold no copy of any of it, so there is nothing on our side to request, export or delete.

6. Data Retention

Your child's profile and progress stay on your device until you delete the profile, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app. Downloaded questions and pictures are cached to keep the games working offline and are refreshed or removed by the app as content changes. We operate no user database, so we retain nothing.

7. Third-Party Services

BrainPlay uses exactly one third-party platform — Google Firebase — and only these parts of it:

  • Cloud Firestore — hosts the question banks the app downloads (read-only)
  • Cloud Storage — hosts the game pictures and animated icons (read-only)
  • App Check with Play Integrity — verifies that requests come from a genuine copy of the app

BrainPlay does not include Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, any advertising network, or any social-media or login SDK.

8. Security

  • All network traffic uses HTTPS
  • Child data lives in the app's private storage, which Android isolates from other apps
  • The parental PIN is stored as a salted SHA-256 hash with an attempt lockout
  • App Check restricts our content to genuine copies of the app
  • The smallest possible attack surface: no accounts, no uploads, and only two permissions

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any change will be reflected in the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. If a future version of BrainPlay ever collects more than it does today, this policy will be updated before that version is released.
