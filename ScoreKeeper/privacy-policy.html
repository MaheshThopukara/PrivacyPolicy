🔒 Privacy Policy

Score Keeper App

Last updated: March 20, 2026

1. Overview

Score Keeper ("the App") is a score tracking application developed by Mahesh. This privacy policy explains how the App handles your data.

Summary: Score Keeper does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. All information stays on your device.

2. Data Collection

Score Keeper does NOT collect, transmit, or share any personal data. The App does not require internet access and cannot communicate with any server.

The App does not use any analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

3. Data Storage

All data — including player names, game scores, and game settings — is stored locally on your device only, using an on-device database.

No data is uploaded to any server, cloud service, or third-party platform.

4. Third-Party Services

Score Keeper uses NO third-party analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or data processing services. There are zero third-party SDKs that collect or process user data.

5. Permissions

Score Keeper requests NO special permissions. It does not access the internet, camera, microphone, location, contacts, storage, or any other sensitive device feature.

6. Children's Privacy

Score Keeper is a family-friendly utility app. It does not collect any personal information from any user, including children under 13. The App is safe for users of all ages.

7. Data Deletion

Users can delete individual games or all stored data at any time within the App. Uninstalling the App also removes all locally stored data from the device.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
